The everyday headache

The document is somewhere. But where?

When subcontractor records live across inboxes, shared drives and spreadsheets, a simple check can turn into a round of chasing.

⌕

Time lost searching

Finding the latest insurance certificate in email threads, folders or someone else's spreadsheet.

◷

Renewals caught too late

Expiry dates are easy to miss when nobody owns a reliable, up-to-date list.

↗

Repeated follow-ups

Remembering who still owes a document, then writing the same request again from scratch.

A simpler workflow

One place to see what needs attention.

SubDoc Tracker is being shaped as a lightweight document register for teams that coordinate subcontractors — not another complex construction management platform.

1

Set the required documents per subcontractor

See at a glance which documents are required, received or still missing.

2

Send a request link — they upload, no account needed

Your subcontractor gets one link, uploads the document and adds the expiry date. Nothing for them to install or log in to.

3

Get reminded before anything expires

Both you and the subcontractor are reminded ahead of the renewal date, so the chasing happens automatically.

Product note: SubDoc Tracker is at discovery / early-access stage. The steps above describe the intended direction, not features already available in a live product.

Who it's for

Built for teams that outgrew the spreadsheet — but not for enterprise software.

A good fit if you…

  • ✓ Are a main contractor coordinating roughly 10–100 subcontractors
  • ✓ Track documents in spreadsheets, email and shared folders today
  • ✓ Need to show a client, auditor or principal designer that everything is in order
  • ✓ Have one or two people who end up doing all the chasing

Probably not for you if…

  • — You already run subcontractor compliance in a full construction platform and it works well
  • — You only work with two or three regular subcontractors
  • — You need a full accreditation or pre-qualification scheme rather than a register
